When you’re done, check your own work against these eight points. They’re also in the exercise brief. One: your decision names a role, a moment and an action — not “clinicians.” Two: every Build Check score has a one-line reason. Three: your verdict follows the rule, and if there’s a Stop, you’ve written what has to change. Four: you chose each pattern from the trigger in the workflow — not from the words in the request. Five: your IDD says what’s out of scope. Six: your matching rule names the field, and what happens when there’s no match. Seven: your metrics cover all three layers — it works, people use it, it changes the outcome. And eight: you found at least three problems in the feed, and each one has a row in your error handling. Six out of eight, and you’re ready for Track B.
