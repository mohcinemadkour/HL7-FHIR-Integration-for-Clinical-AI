Here’s what you hand in. Four deliverables, all for the same request. One: a Build Decision Record — your five Build Check scores, the math, and a verdict. Two: a pattern map — every data flow the solution needs, with one pattern each and the reason. Three: an Interface Design Document — all eight sections, kept to a few pages. And four, the new part: a feed review. You’ll open a small sample of real HL7 v2 discharge messages, find what’s wrong with them, and feed what you find back into your design. The first three use the templates from Module 1. The fourth is a preview of Track B.
