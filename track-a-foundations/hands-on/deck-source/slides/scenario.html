THE REQUEST · LAKESIDE REGIONAL (FICTIONAL)

“We want an AI model that flags patients at high risk of 30-day readmission, so our team can call them after discharge. Can you plug it into Epic?”

CAPACITY

~25 calls a day
~120 discharges a day

WORKFLOW

Nurses work from a call list in a spreadsheet

CONSTRAINTS

3-month interface backlog. No model owner named.

Here’s the request. It’s fictional, but it’s the kind you’ll actually get. The care-transitions director at Lakeside Regional wants a model that flags patients at high risk of readmission within 30 days, so her team can call them after discharge. And she asks: can you plug it into Epic? Here’s what you learn on a 30-minute call. Two nurses make about 25 calls a day, out of about 120 discharges. They work from a call list in a spreadsheet, not from the chart. The interface team has a three-month backlog. And nobody has said who would own the model after go-live. The full scenario is in Resource 3. Read it carefully — every detail matters. And notice the wording of the request. “Plug it into Epic” is a solution. Your job is to find out whether it’s the right one.
