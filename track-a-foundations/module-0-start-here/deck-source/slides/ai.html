LEARNING IN THE AGE OF AI

What AI can and can’t do for you

AI is great at

  • Explaining an OBX segment
  • Listing the fields of a FHIR Observation
  • Drafting a first Mirth transformer

AI can’t give you

  • Why your A08 feed duplicates patients
  • Which mapping survives the next Epic upgrade
  • The judgment of having fixed it in production
Let’s talk about AI for a second, because it changes how you should learn this material. Tools like Claude or ChatGPT are genuinely good at explaining HL7 and FHIR. Ask one what an OBX segment is, or what fields a FHIR Observation has, and you’ll get a solid answer. It can even draft a first version of a Mirth transformer for you. I use these tools every day, and you should too. But here’s what they can’t give you. They can’t tell you why your ADT A08 updates are creating duplicate patients in your feature store. They can’t tell you which mapping choice will break when the hospital upgrades Epic next year. And they can’t give you the instinct you only get from having fixed these things in production. That gap — between knowing the definition and making the right call on a real interface — is exactly what this course is designed to close.
