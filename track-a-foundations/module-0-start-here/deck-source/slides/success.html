So how will you know this course worked for you? Not by the completion certificate. You’ll know it worked when you can look at a raw HL7 message and immediately spot the problem — the wrong encoding, the missing segment, the unit that will break your model. You’ll know it worked when you can sit across from a hospital interface team and defend your design: why FHIR here, why HL7 v2 there, why this mapping and not that one. And you’ll know it worked when you can take a model from a notebook all the way to an alert in front of a clinician, end to end. And if you disagree with how I did something in this course — good. Tell me in the Q&A. Some of the best improvements to this course will come from students who pushed back.
