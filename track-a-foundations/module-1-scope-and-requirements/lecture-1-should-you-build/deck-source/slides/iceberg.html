Here’s the picture I want you to keep in your head for the rest of the course. The build is the part everyone sees: the Mirth channel, the mappings, the model wrapper, the testing. It gets a budget and a project plan. The run is underneath, and it never ends. Every EHR upgrade can change the feed. Someone will ask for one more field. Messages will fail, and someone gets paged at two in the morning. Security will review it every year. And here’s the part that’s specific to clinical AI: you don’t have one thing aging, you have two. The interface ages, and the model ages. Patient populations shift, lab assays change, documentation habits change — and your model drifts. That means monitoring, revalidation and sometimes retraining, for as long as it’s live.
