Cost of the problem, per year

events/day × minutes lost × 365 ÷ 60 × hourly cost

Example: 40 × 6 × 365 ÷ 60 = 1,460 h
1,460 h × $60 = $87,600 / year

Cost of the integration, 5 years

build + 5 × run per year

Your numbers: [$ build] + 5 × [$ run]
= [$ total]

Example inputs are illustrative — replace them with yours. Safety and compliance risks don’t fit in hours: list them next to the number.
