THE REAL QUESTION

Not “Can we connect it?”
Not “Does Epic support FHIR for this?”

What decision will this change — and what will it cost to keep running for five years?

Let’s start with the question almost nobody asks at the start of an integration project. When a request lands on your desk — “can we get the lab results into the model in real time?” — the conversation usually jumps straight to feasibility. Can we connect to it? Does Epic expose this through FHIR? Is there an HL7 feed we can tap? Those are good questions. They’re just not the first ones. The first questions are these two. One: what clinical or operational decision will this change? If you can’t name the decision and the person who makes it, you don’t have a use case yet — you have a data plumbing project. Two: what will it cost to keep this running for five years? Not to build it. To keep it running. Because in a hospital, that’s where the money and the pain actually go. Let’s look at that second question first.
