THE REAL QUESTION

Not “Can we connect it?”
Not “Does Epic support FHIR for this?”

What decision will this change — and what will it cost to keep running for five years?

• The question almost nobody asks at the start. • Not “Can we connect it?” Not “Does Epic support FHIR for this?” • First: what decision will this change, and who makes it? • No decision and no person? Then it's a plumbing project, not a use case. • Second: what will it cost to keep running for five years? • Not to build it. To run it. That's where the money and the pain go. • Let's start with that second question.
