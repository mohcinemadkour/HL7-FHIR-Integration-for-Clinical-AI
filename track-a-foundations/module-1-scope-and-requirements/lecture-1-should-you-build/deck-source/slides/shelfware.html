Now the first question: what decision does it change? Here’s a failure pattern you’ll see again and again in clinical AI, and it never shows up in an incident report. On the left is the pipeline dashboard. The feed is up. The model scores every patient every hour. The results are written back to the EHR as FHIR resources. Zero errors in thirty days. From an engineering point of view, it’s a success. On the right is the question nobody checked: who acted on the last score? Because if the score lands in a tab nobody opens, or in an inbox nobody reads, or arrives after the decision has already been made — the pipeline changed nothing. It’s just an expensive way to store numbers.
