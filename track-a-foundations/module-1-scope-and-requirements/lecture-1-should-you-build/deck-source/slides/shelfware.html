Here’s a failure you’ll never see in an incident report. On the left, the pipeline is perfect: the feed is up, the model scores every hour, zero errors in thirty days. On the right is the question nobody asked: who acted on the last score? If the score lands where nobody looks, or after the decision is made, the pipeline changed nothing. So we start from the decision, not from the data.
