Let’s check your answers. Case A, the ICU transfer: an HL7 v2 feed. It’s an event, and you need to react the moment it happens. Specifically, it’s an ADT A02 — a patient transfer message. Case B, the risk-trend app: SMART App Launch. A nurse opens it from the chart, logs in, and the app gets the patient context from the EHR. Case C, retraining: Bulk export. Two years of encounters for the whole hospital is a population-level job, run with backend services. Please don’t try this with thousands of individual FHIR queries — you’ll hit rate limits, and the interface team will not be happy with you. Case D, the drug interaction warning: CDS Hooks, on the order-sign hook. It’s advice at a specific moment in the workflow, before the order is final. If you got all four, great.
