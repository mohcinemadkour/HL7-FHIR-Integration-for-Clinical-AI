Choose your pattern

HL7 v2, FHIR, Bulk, SMART, CDS Hooks or documents — matched to the workflow, not the hype

Mohcine Madkour, PhD · HL7 FHIR Integration for Clinical AI

Welcome to lecture 2. In the last lecture, you decided whether to build. Now, for the requests that passed the Build Check, we answer the next question: which pattern? Here’s the mistake I see most often. A team says “we’re doing a FHIR integration” before anyone has described the workflow. FHIR is a great standard, but it isn’t one thing — and it isn’t always the answer. Inside most hospitals, a lot of the real-time clinical data still moves as HL7 v2 messages. In this lecture, you’ll learn the six patterns you’ll meet in clinical AI work, a quick way to pick between them, and how one real pipeline — our sepsis project — uses several of them together. It’s about fifteen minutes. There’s a short exercise at the end, so keep a notepad close.
