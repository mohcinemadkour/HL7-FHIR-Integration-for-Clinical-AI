THE SIX PATTERNS

Where each pattern lives

Here are the six patterns you’ll meet, placed on one map. The vertical axis is freshness: real-time at the top, on demand in the middle, periodic at the bottom. The horizontal axis is scope: one patient on the left, the whole population on the right. Top left, in orange: CDS Hooks. Real-time, one patient, right inside the clinician’s workflow. For clinical AI, this is where your prediction meets a human decision. Top, toward the middle: the HL7 v2 feed. Real-time events — admissions, transfers, lab results — streaming for every patient in the hospital as they happen. On the left, in teal: the SMART on FHIR app. A person opens it, for one patient, when they need it. In the middle: the plain FHIR query. Your system asks for one patient’s data on demand. Lower left: the C-CDA document — a snapshot of one patient at one moment, usually sent between organizations. And bottom right: FHIR Bulk Data export. Thousands of patients, on a schedule. That’s your training and monitoring data. Keep this map in mind. Every pattern we discuss today has a place on it.
