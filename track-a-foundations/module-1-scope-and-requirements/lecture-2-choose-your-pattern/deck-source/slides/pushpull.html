THE CORE DIFFERENCE

HL7 v2 pushes. FHIR waits to be asked.

• The one technical idea to remember: push vs. pull. • HL7 v2 is push. Something happens — the EHR sends a message. • It goes over MLLP to Mirth, gets transformed, reaches your model. • Reliable and mature. But formats are rigid and vary by site. • FHIR REST is pull. You ask, the server answers with a Bundle. • Great for on-demand access. But you don't know when something changed. • FHIR Subscriptions exist, but support varies. Check your site first. • Push for events. Pull for questions. Most pipelines need both.
