Before we look at any standard, here are the three questions that pick the pattern for you. One: what triggers the data movement? Is it an event — a patient is admitted, a lab result is finalized? Is it a request — your app asks for data when it needs it? Is it a schedule — every night? Or is it a specific moment in the clinician’s workflow, like opening a chart or signing an order? Two: who’s in the loop? Is it a system talking to a system with no human involved, or is there a person who logs in and looks at the data? Three: how many patients per request? One patient right now, or thousands of patients at once? Notice that none of these questions mentions HL7 or FHIR. They’re about the workflow. You answered most of them already in the Build Check last lecture — the decision, the person and the moment. Now those answers pick the technology.
