WORKED EXAMPLE · OUR SEPSIS PIPELINE

One product, four patterns

• Our sepsis pipeline uses four patterns. • Top row, real time: EHR pushes HL7 v2 to Mirth. • Mirth writes FHIR resources to our FHIR server. • The model queries FHIR for features — backend services, no human. • The result reaches the clinician as a CDS Hooks card. • Bottom row, nightly: Bulk export for training and drift monitoring. • “Is it HL7 or FHIR?” Usually: both, for different flows. • You document each flow's pattern in the IDD — next lecture.
