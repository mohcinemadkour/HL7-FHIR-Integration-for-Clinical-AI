• Ask in this order. Stop at the first yes. • 1 · Alert at a workflow moment? → CDS Hooks. • 2 · A person opens an app and logs in? → SMART App Launch. • 3 · Thousands of patients at once? → Bulk export. • 4 · Fixed snapshot to another organization? → C-CDA. • 5 · React the moment something happens? → HL7 v2 feed. • None of these? → FHIR query with backend services. • People and scale come first — they're hardest to change later. • This gives the pattern for one data flow. Real products have several.
