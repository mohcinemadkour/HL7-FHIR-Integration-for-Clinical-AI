• Section 6: constraints vs. assumptions. • Constraints are fixed. HIPAA, TLS, what the EHR supports, budget. • Assumptions look like facts until go-live. • Sepsis: vitals arrive within minutes. Lactate is usually there. Nurses see the card. • Any one wrong, and the project fails with perfect code. • Give every assumption an owner and a test. • Test the riskiest first — before you build.
