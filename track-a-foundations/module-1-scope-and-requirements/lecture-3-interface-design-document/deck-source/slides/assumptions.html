Section six separates two things teams often mix up. Constraints are fixed. You accept them and design around them. HIPAA and encryption everywhere. Maybe the EHR only supports CDS Hooks on patient-view. Maybe there’s no budget for a new interface engine. Assumptions are different. They’re things you believe are true but haven’t verified. And they look exactly like facts — until go-live. For sepsis: we assume vitals reach Mirth within a few minutes. We assume lactate is usually present when we score. We assume nurses will see the card at the vitals check. Any of these can be wrong, and if it is, the project fails even though every line of code is correct. So every assumption in the IDD gets two things: an owner and a test. Measure thirty days of feed timestamps. Check a de-identified sample for lactate. Shadow one shift and watch how the nurses actually work. Test the riskiest assumptions first — ideally before you build, not after.
